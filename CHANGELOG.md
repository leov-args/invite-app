## Unreleased

### Added
- Vite + TypeScript scaffold: `vite.config.ts` with `VITE_BASE` (default `/invite-app/`), `asset()` helper, `public/.nojekyll`, versioned `package-lock.json` and `npm run check`; assets moved to `public/assets/`, `paleta.png` to `docs/` (E1-1).
- Design tokens (`tokens.css`: SPEC §4.1 palette plus `--c-blanco`, §4.3 shape and spacing, §4.4 motion, font stacks), seven self-hosted `@font-face` rules with `font-display: swap`, `base.css` (480 px column, fixed ambient background and grain, mobile baseline) and `content.ts` with the §3 data and its test; temporary type/palette test page (E1-2).
- Build-time HTML metadata from `content.ts` (`<title>`, description, absolute `og:*` with 1200×630 image) via an inline Vite plugin and `src/meta.ts`; static `noindex`, `theme-color` and SVG favicon; `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` to GitHub Pages running `npm run check` on Node 24 (E1-3).
- Opening screen (SPEC §5.0): static night overlay in `index.html` with a warm halo, phrase and "Abrir invitación" button (texts in `content.textos.apertura`), dissolve into light, CSS scroll lock with `inert` main, `sessionStorage` memory with a no-flash inline head script, keyboard focus ring and reduced-motion fades; `--dur-pulsar` press token; `src/modules/opening.ts` and `src/styles/sections.css` (E2-1).
- `apps-script/Code.gs`, the RSVP receiver for the Google Sheet copied verbatim from SPEC §5.9.1, plus `apps-script/code.test.ts`, which fails `npm run check` if it drifts from the SPEC (E6-1).
- Background music (SPEC § "Elemento persistente"): `src/modules/music.ts` with a lazily created `<audio>` → `MediaElementAudioSourceNode` → `GainNode` graph started in the opening gesture, gain fades (1.5 s start and loop seam, 800 ms toggle), `visibilitychange` pause/resume, and a 44 px floating blur button with animated bars, dynamic `aria-label` from `content.textos.musica`, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency variants (E2-2).
- Hero (SPEC §5.1): static section with a circular portrait (`<picture>` webp/jpg via `asset()`), 3 px turquesa and 1 px plata rings, white halo, 12 s slow zoom that starts on opening, "EN MEMORIA DE" label and two-line name from `content.ts`, turquoise separator and pulsing silver chevron, reduced-motion variant; `src/modules/hero.ts` (E3-1).
- "Su vocación" section (SPEC §5.2): turquesa-suave → agua-claro band with a 48 px medical cross, "SU VOCACIÓN" title, phrase, overlapped graduation pair (02/03) in white 8 px frames rotated −2°/+2°, full-width photos 04 and 05, Spanish alts and optional captions from `content.textos.vocacion`, and the blurred stethoscope bleeding from the bottom-right corner as a new 440 px `estetoscopio-desenfocado.webp` (11 KB instead of the 207 KB PNG); `src/modules/vocacion.ts` (E3-2).
- On-scroll reveal (SPEC §4.4): `src/modules/reveal.ts` marks hero and vocation content with `.reveal` from JS and reveals it through one `IntersectionObserver` that fires before elements enter the viewport; text fades and rises 16 px over `--dur-texto`, photos also go from blur 8 px and scale 1.04 over `--dur-foto`, staggered 250 ms per batch; reveals wait for the opening overlay; reduced motion keeps only a 300 ms fade (E3-3).
- Message section (SPEC §5.3): full-bleed marfil band with soft diagonal warm light rays (a `::before` layer at 17–25 % opacity that breathes over 9 s, static under reduced motion), the §5.3 phrase in Cormorant italic from `content.textos.mensaje`, and the lazy `flores-borde.webp` edge to edge at the foot; text and flowers join the on-scroll reveal; `src/modules/mensaje.ts` (E4-1).
- Mass card (SPEC §5.4, details only): full-bleed bruma band with a white 85 % card (`--c-plata-claro` border, card radius, soft shadow) holding the lazy `cruz-luz.png`, the "MISA EN SU MEMORIA" label from `content.textos.misa`, the turquoise separator, the date in Cormorant 500 and time, place and address lines with thin turquoise clock, church and pin icons; any empty value hides its line without leaving a gap; the card joins the on-scroll reveal; `src/modules/misa.ts` (E4-2).
- Mass countdown (SPEC §5.4): pure `estado(ahora, inicio)` in `src/modules/countdown.ts` with `countdown.test.ts` (Lima 10:59:59, 11:00, 13:59:59 and 14:00, also under `TZ=Europe/Madrid`); the card shows days, hours, minutes and seconds updated every second, then "Hoy nos reunimos en su memoria" from 11:00 to 14:00 Lima and "Gracias por acompañarnos" afterwards, with texts in `content.textos.misa` (E4-3).
- Add to calendar (SPEC §5.4): pure `ics()` and `googleCalendarUrl()` in `src/modules/calendar.ts` with `calendar.test.ts` (`DTSTART:20270116T160000Z`, `DTEND:20270116T170000Z`, no VTIMEZONE, CRLF, RFC 5545 escaping and 75-octet folding, also under `TZ=Europe/Madrid`); the mass card gains a primary "Agregar al calendario" pill that downloads the `.ics` and an outline "Google Calendar" pill with the same UTC hours (E4-4).
- Mass location (SPEC §5.5, map only): "DÓNDE NOS REUNIREMOS" section after the mass card with the place name and address, and a lazy Google Maps iframe without an API key (4:3, 16:9 from 600 px, desaturated, decorative turquoise pin on the center); pure `mapEmbedUrl()` in `src/modules/location.ts` picks embedUrl, then lat/lng, then placeQuery, with `location.test.ts`; empty data hides its line, the map or the whole section; the mass card gains an outline "Ver ubicación" pill that smooth-scrolls to it (instant under reduced motion) (E5-1).
